Identity

October 23, 2026 — a quick journal entry

Identity → action → outcome

The chain I keep coming back to is identity → action → outcome. Outcomes don't come from trying harder at the actions — the actions come from who you believe you are. If I see myself as a runner, the runs happen. If I see myself as "someone trying to run", every run is a negotiation. Work backwards from the outcome you want and the question becomes: what kind of person does that effortlessly?

Psycho-Cybernetics

I read Psycho-Cybernetics too long ago to quote it properly, but it's an interesting book about self-image and it touches on exactly this. The core idea stuck with me: you act consistently with the picture you hold of yourself, so the picture is the thing to fix first. Change the self-image and the behaviour follows — willpower optional. I want to reread it with this framework in mind.

Alter egos and larping

Recently I've seen a lot of content on Instagram about building an alter ego and larping — deliberately acting as the person you want to become, even before you feel like them. Influencers make really big claims about the benefits, and I'm sceptical of the hype but curious about the kernel of truth underneath. Fake it till you make it has been around forever; the question is whether it actually rewires identity or just feels like a trick.

More to come

I'm going to return to this and expand on it after doing more research — rereading Psycho-Cybernetics, digging into the alter-ego claims, and testing a small version of it myself. For now, the chain is the note: identity first, action second, outcome last.
