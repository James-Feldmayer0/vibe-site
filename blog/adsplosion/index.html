Adsplosion Post-Mortem

October 22, 2026 — design notes on /adsplosion

Concept: Ad Parody Game

I thought it would be funny to make a game where we bombard the user with ads as part of the core gameplay mechanic.

I like the concept of having the score as my actual earned ad revenue. I don't think that's a compelling user experience. It's important to me that the game is fun to play.

I love the joke of defeating ads and the final boss being Adsplosion itself.

I enjoy exploring what Adsplosion could mean:

  • Is Adsplosion a version of the AI singularity that is perfectly aligned with corporate interests?
    • Adsplosion loses when the users become sufficiently educated and motivated to insulate themselves from ad technology
    • I like the joke of the bots still serving ads to each other after the humans leave but that's not perfect alignment
  • Adsplosion could be an anti-ad terror organisation
    • Satire about how using ad blocker makes you a terrorist (and supporting Palestine makes you a double terrorist)
    • I think this is superficial

What didn't work

I chose to use fruit merge mechanics because I like the idea of the screen filling with ads. The style of video ads that I chose to use don't read well at these sizes and there are technical challenges around having video textures with physics in the browser.

I do enjoy the concept. But I need to align it with a satisfing gameplay loop that allows the jokes to land.

“Lose 30 lbs by doing nothing! Results not typical. Results not real.”
Grandma’s secret to a flat stomach (the secret is a lawsuit)
AI-powered toothbrush: “Now with subscription”
NFT of a cookie banner

Fake interface tricks

A close button that is actually a second ad
An X so tiny it needs a magnifier, and the magnifier is also an ad
“Skip Ad in 5” that goes to 1, then resets to 10
A “Sponsored” label that is itself sponsored by another sponsor
A fake “Virus detected!” alert with a fake progress bar
A fake Windows error dialog whose “OK” button does nothing
A cookie banner with “Accept All” in huge green and “Reject” as a single gray pixel
A “close” button that moves away like a cursed cartoon
A “Your call is important to us” hold-music popup

Urgency and manipulation

“Only 1 left! (We have 40,000 of them.)”
A countdown timer that is clearly just a random number generator
“12 people are viewing this right now” (the number is always 12)
“Wait! Before you go...” popups that appear when you stay
A guilt-trip decline button: “No thanks, I enjoy being poor”
“You are visitor #1,000,000! Claim your prize!” (you’re always visitor #1,000,000)

Escalating tiers, funniest at the end

Tracking pixel, which is invisible but has a tiny “$0.0001” popup
Cookie banner
Newsletter popup (“Join 3 other subscribers”)
Unskippable video ad about skipping ads
“You’ve won an iPhone!” full-screen ad
Final boss: an ad for the game itself, “Adsplosion, now with more ads”

Sound and feel

Cash register ding on every payout
Ads that autoplay a muffled, distant 2008 jingle
Notification sounds from apps you don’t own
A dial-up modem screech for a big combo
A “Sponsored” voice that says “this score brought to you by...” mid-game

Meta jokes (the best ones, I think)

The game interrupts itself with a fake ad for a fake game
A pause menu that says “Watch an ad to unpause”
A game-over screen: “Watch an ad to continue (it’s the same ad you just watched)”
A “Remove ads for $4.99” button that adds more ads
A settings toggle labeled “Ad personalization” that makes the ads more personal (“Hey Dave, still thinking about that toaster?”)
A fake loading bar that stalls at 99% and offers premium to skip the wait
