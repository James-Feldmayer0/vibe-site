Putting in Effort to Engage with Strangers

October 24, 2026 — a quick journal entry

The experiment

Today I made an extra effort to be friendly and engage with strangers. Not a grand social challenge, just a deliberate bit of warmth: greetings, small talk, actually being present with the people around me instead of moving through the day with my head down.

Everyone was lovely back

And here's the thing — everyone was super lovely back to me. It was really energising. Every small exchange seemed to put a bit more in the tank than it took out. I'd forgotten how good it feels to just be warm with people and savour those small social interactions instead of rushing past them.

The commitment

I think making a commitment to being warm and savouring those small social interactions every day will do wonders for my mental health. And maybe it will lead to some fantastic new opportunities — building new friendships, inspiring me to try new things, and seeing new places.

The payoffs I'm hoping for:

  • Friendship. I would love to establish myself in this community. Friendship and community is a core pillar of building the beautiful life.
  • New things. Strangers recommend the courses, the cafés, the classes and the events you'd never find on your own.
  • Energy. Warm, small interactions compound — each one made the next one easier.

That's the experiment. Say the thing, ask the question, be the friendly one. Small daily effort, big compounding returns.
