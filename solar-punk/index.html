Solar Punk

An autonomous fruit-picking drone that drinks sunlight all day and picks peaches all afternoon. No fuel. No cords. No compressed-gas refills.

🛸

Why solar punk

“It works dawn to dusk, parks itself in a tree at night, and the orchard ends each season healthier than it started.”
— design brief, 2026

Features

☀️

Sun-powered everything

High-density flexible solar skin over the rotors' frame harvests enough juice for a full picking shift. Battery surplus trickles back into the orchard's irrigation controllers at night.

🤏

Three-finger soft gripper

Compliant silicone fingers sense contact pressure at 1 kHz, so every apple, peach, and plum comes off the branch bruise-free — sorted and dropped gently into the padded harvest bin.

🌳

Canopy-aware flight

Stereo vision + LIDAR builds a 3D map of each tree, plans rip lines through the branches, and navigates without a single snapped twig. It learns each row as it goes.

🩺

Orchard health scouting

While it picks, it watches. Multispectral imaging flags pest stress, water deficits, and early blight — routing alerts to your phone before you'd have spotted them on foot.

🌙

Parks itself at dusk

When the sun goes down, the drone perches on a docked roost station — no hangar required — and uploads the day's harvest map while its cells trickle-charge for sunrise.

🛠️

Repairable by design

Modular arms, swappable battery pack, published schematics. A broken rotor arm is a 10-minute field fix, not a warranty claim.
